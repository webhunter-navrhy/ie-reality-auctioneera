Auctioneera is delighted to present 47 Cedarwood Avenue, Carrigaline.

Nestled in the charming locale of Carrigaline, this is a delightful detached residence boasting two bedrooms and two bathrooms. Exhibiting contemporary architectural style, the property is accented by a neat front façade with a tidy lawn bordered by plants, providing a welcoming entrance. A private driveway ensures convenient off-street parking.

Upon entering, the hallway leads into a spacious living room, illuminated by a large window that offers a view of the front garden. The room's layout comfortably accommodates various seating arrangements, perfect for relaxation or entertaining. Beyond the living room lies a well-proportioned kitchen featuring ample countertop space and cabinetry, perfect for culinary endeavours. The kitchen's design allows dining and sitting area, making it a versatile heart of the home.

The accommodation is completed by two comfortable bedrooms. The master bedroom is generously sized and benefits from an ensuite bathroom, a valuable asset for privacy and convenience. The second bedroom is also well-sized, with a window overlooking the rear of the property, ensuring a peaceful atmosphere. The main bathroom is centrally located for additional accessibility.

Property also features inviting screened porch, offering a peaceful space to relax, entertain, and enjoy the fresh air. Whether you’re sipping your morning coffee, hosting guests, or unwinding in the evening, this versatile porch provides a charming place in the home and a perfect connection to the outdoors.

At the back, the property includes a large private garden, ideal for outdoor dining or gardening enthusiasts, ensuring a balance of indoor and outdoor living. Situated near main road networks, this property is conveniently located within proximity to local schools, shops, and community facilities. Public transport links are readily available, with bus stops a short stroll away, making commuting straightforward.

This home is ideal for first-time buyers seeking a welcoming community, empty nesters looking to downsize, or investors wishing to expand their portfolio with a manageabale property in a sought-after area. The practical layout and location offer a blend of comfort and convenience, meeting diverse lifestyle needs.
